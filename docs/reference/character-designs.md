# character design reference sheet (user-supplied)

A grid of ~17 character marks, described here for provenance (image supplied in
conversation). Complements `avatar-prompt.md`.

## What the sheet shows

- **Solid black chunky silhouettes** — capsule figures, blobs, a heart (CDG Play), a
  house, a hat/UFO, a key, a flame, a flower, a figure-8, a bird, a penguin-ish walker,
  a sun-spike creature. Objects-as-characters are common: anything chunky can be an
  inhabitant.
- **Eyes carry everything.** Large white cutouts with solid dark pupils. Crucially the
  pupils are usually **off-center — glancing sideways** — which is where the deadpan
  personality comes from. Some marks have one eye (flame, flower, key, sun-spike),
  many have two.
- **Tiny stubby legs** on otherwise legless forms (house with legs, circle with legs,
  the arch with drooping feet).
- **Edge quality varies** from crisp vector to crayon/charcoal fuzz — both read as the
  same family. Fuzzy edges echo the avatar prompt's "grainy, stippled, slightly
  irregular."
- One frame is the single warm accent (flame on vermilion) — consistent with the retired
  accent's eventual return, not a reason to reintroduce it now.

## Standing rulings that this sheet does NOT change

- **One eye always** (explicit user instruction) — the sheet's two-eyed marks are style
  reference, not a revocation.
- Black and white only.

## Adopted from this sheet

- **Glancing pupils**: the pupil sits off-center and drifts — seeded slow gaze wander at
  minimum, look-at targets from behavior later.
- Chunky proportions and stubby legs confirmed as the species default (already in the
  avatar spec and blend-shell plan).
- Object-like silhouettes are legitimate creatures — no need to coerce drawings toward
  animal anatomy when they read as a house or a key; legs + eye are enough.
